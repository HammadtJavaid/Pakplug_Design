## 1. Overview

**Goal.** A driver standing at a charger goes from "I'm here" to "charging" in under 20 seconds, with one honest promise of what they'll get. The flow:

1. **Charge tab** shows what matters right now: the next booking (and whether it's startable yet), one big **Scan QR code** action, and quiet shortcuts (enter the code, find a charger, your vehicle).
2. **Identify the charger**: scan its QR, or type its 6-character code. The app runs the logic's booking and ±15-minute window checks.
3. **Charge planner** (new, Hammad's ask) [CH3–CH5]: PakPlug can't read the car's battery, so it **asks** "What's your battery now?" (big % slider + presets, vehicle shown and switchable, or skip). Then the driver plans **by time** (15 min … until the slot ends) or **by target %**, and a live **Estimate** shows energy (kWh), cost (Rs), the end battery % and the finish time. It is always labelled "Estimate. The charger's meter decides the final amount."
4. **Slide to start charging** (approved presentation change, design spec §4.1-3) → **Connecting to charger** → Live session (Flow 06).

The planner is the logic's `StartSessionDialog` grown up: its Station, Connector and Rate rows and its "Start charging session?" title are all still there, and its Start / Cancel become Slide to start / ✕.

### 1.1 Dummy maths (the brief's story; Rayan swaps in the backend later)

Shown in a notes card next to 05.23 (Caption 1, text/secondary):
- `kWh = charger kW × hours × 0.92`, capped by `(target% − start%) × battery kWh`.
- `% added = kWh ÷ battery kWh` · `cost = kWh × Rs/kWh` (per-kWh) or `hours × Rs/hr` (per-hour).
- `finish = now + hours` (time plan) or `now + needed kWh ÷ (kW × 0.92)` (target plan), never later than the slot end (the logic auto-stops at the slot end).

AC story: **GreenVolt · DHA Phase 5**, Type 2 7.4 kW, Rs 42/kWh, booking Today, Sat 4 Oct · 6:00 → 7:00 PM, BYD Atto 3 (60.5 kWh), starting at 6:00 PM at 20% (= 12.1 kWh in the pack). 7.4 × 0.92 = 6.808 kWh per hour.

- Curves: `snappy` = SwiftUI `.snappy` (response 0.30, damping 0.85; Flutter stiffness 440, damping 36), settles ≈ 300 ms · `smooth` = `.smooth` (0.45, 1.0; Flutter 195 / 28), ≈ 450 ms · `bouncy` = `.bouncy` (0.40, 0.70; Flutter 250 / 22), ≈ 400 ms with one ≈ 4% overshoot · `fade.quick` 150 ms ease-out · `fade.std` 220 ms ease-out.
- Press feedback (not repeated per rule): `Button / Large` and `Button / Medium` swap to Pressed on touch-down and scale 0.97 (`snappy`); glass `Icon Button` (back, ✕, torch) scale 0.92 + fill brightens 6%; cards (Next booking, Scan card, Session card) scale 0.98, a drag > 10pt cancels; chips scale 0.96. Disabled variants: no press, no haptic. Reduce Motion: no scale, the element dims to 85% for the touch.
- **Haptics fire on release (the action), never on touch-down.** Map: selection = chips, presets, segmented, slider ticks, toggles · light = navigation, card taps, sheet open · medium = commits (Slide to start, Continue on the Charger found sheet) · success = booking found, charger found, session started · warning = "not yet / can't" states the driver can fix · error = something failed.
- Toast (every logic snackbar in this flow): enters from 12pt below (`smooth`), leaves `fade.quick`; 4 s for one line without action, 6 s for two lines or any action; swipe down dismisses; one at a time; haptic follows tone (Error → error, Warning → warning, Neutral → none); VoiceOver posts the message as a polite announcement without moving focus, action via the Actions rotor, timer pauses while VoiceOver focus is inside; Reduce Motion: fade only.
- Native alert: scrim `fade.std`; alert scales 1.08 → 1 with opacity 0 → 1 (250 ms ease-out, the iOS alert motion, same as 02.04); dismiss `fade.quick`.
- Sheets: rise with `smooth` (≈ 450 ms) to the medium detent, scrim `fade.std`; the screen behind does not scale (00-system: only a sheet stacked on another sheet recedes to 0.96, as 04.35); swipe-down or scrim tap dismisses unless stated.
- Reduce Transparency: every Frost Strong card and glass control becomes Surface + hairline; the scanner mask becomes solid black 80%. Low Power Mode: loops (breathing, pulse, shimmer, sweep) stop; state changes still show.
- VoiceOver conventions (02): titles carry the heading trait; status after the name; "≈" is read "about"; prices read "42 rupees per kilowatt-hour". Every VoiceOver string in this spec is a new accessibility label [CH24].

### 4.0 Additions to the system tokens (for 00-system)
- `roll`: numeric digit roll. SwiftUI `.contentTransition(.numericText(value:))` inside `withAnimation(.snappy(duration: 0.22))`; Flutter: per-digit `AnimatedSwitcher` with a vertical slide, 220 ms, `Cubic(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (00-system `roll`). Digits move up when the value grows and down when it shrinks; unchanged digits don't move; the "≈", "Rs" and units never roll. Reduce Motion: 150 ms cross-fade.
- `scrub` (00-system; named `tick` in the draft, renamed because 06's `tick` is the 1 s clock): selection haptic every 5% on any % slider and every step on duration chips; never more than one per 60 ms (fast drags don't buzz).
- `radiate` (00-system; named `pulse` in the draft, renamed because `pulse` is 06's 1.33 s dot ring): Connecting rings, 1.8 s loop, scale 1 → 1.35, opacity 0.35 → 0, ease-out, rings staggered 600 ms. Off in Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode.

## 8. Edge cases & defaults chosen (Hammad asleep: decided)

1. **What's under the planner.** On a successful scan or confirmed code, the scanner / code screen is **replaced** (not stacked), so ✕ on the planner lands on the Charge tab. Rescanning is one tap from there. (The logic's dialog sat on top of the scanner; Cancel returned to it. Returning to a live camera after cancelling was judged worse.)
2. **Battery never pre-filled.** Unset every time (CH3). If the app was killed mid-planner, nothing started; the next scan starts fresh.
3. **Battery at 100%.** Allowed. Time mode shows "0 kWh · Rs 0" with the Estimate message "Your battery is already full. The car will draw little or no power." Slide stays enabled (the driver may know better, e.g. preconditioning).
4. **Battery 0%.** Allowed. The 0% end gives a light haptic.
5. **Target ≤ battery now** → 05.27 (Unavailable + Slide disabled). Presets at or below the start are dimmed.
6. **Plan longer than the slot.** Time mode: chips past the slot end are dimmed (can't exceed). Target mode: capped at the slot end (05.25); the logic auto-stops at the slot end.
7. **Started early** (within the 15-min window, e.g. 5:50 for 6:00). "Until 7:00 PM" = 1 h 10 min (the logic auto-stops only at the slot end). Chips are computed from *now*.
8. **Started late** (6:20). "Until 7:00 PM · 40 min" is the default; 45 min and longer are dimmed. With < 15 min left only "Until 7:00 PM · 8 min" shows, plus the Estimate line "Your slot ends in 8 min." This follows logic §D (only "too early (more than 15 min)" and "expired" are rejected, so a late start is allowed until the slot end). Appendix 4 calls the rule a "±15-minute time window"; if the code really rejects starts more than 15 min after the slot start, late starts get the "ended" alert instead and this edge case disappears (FLAG for Rayan).
9. **Unknown battery** (skip) → 05.28; Target mode dimmed. Live shows energy and cost without % (06).
10. **Vehicle without battery size** → kWh and cost only; "Add battery size" → Edit vehicle → back to the planner with the value applied.
11. **Vehicle doesn't match the connector** (the logic has no check at start). The planner shows a warning line in the summary row "BYD Atto 3 doesn't list Type 2 (AC)" but doesn't block (logic unchanged).
12. **Booking's vehicle deleted** → planner falls back to the primary vehicle; none → time-only estimate and "Add vehicle".
13. **Charger kW unknown** → Estimate Unavailable "We can't estimate this charger yet."; start still allowed. **Rate unknown** → Cost "—" and summary "Rs — / kWh" (logic).
14. **Per-hour stations** → Cost = hours × Rs/hr (05.30). Note for Rayan: the logic's live "SO FAR" is already time × hourly rate for every station, while the planner uses kWh × Rs/kWh on per-kWh stations; the backend must decide which applies (FLAG).
15. **Peak pricing** (booking shows "Peak 5 PM–9 PM") → the dummy uses one flat rate; the real estimate must split the plan by hourly rate (FLAG).
16. **DC taper** → dummy: full power to 80%, half power above 80% (only matters for 90–100% targets); a DC 20 → 100% plan says "≈1 h 6 min" (36.3 kWh at 55.2 kWh/h + 12.1 kWh at 27.6 kWh/h).
17. **Two bookings at one station** → the logic picks the earliest confirmed booking whose end is after now; the planner uses that slot.
18. **Booking at another station** → "no booking at this station" alert (05.11), with "Book a slot".
19. **Session already active** → Charge tab State 2; the scanner isn't reachable. A deep link into the scanner while charging shows the State 2 content instead.
20. **Double scans** → the logic's `_isProcessing` guard plus a 1.5 s per-payload cooldown after errors.
21. **Start request in flight when the app is killed** → on relaunch the shell hydrates any active session (logic) and shows Live; if none, the Charge tab.
22. **Clock rolls over while the planner is open** → plans re-anchor each minute (4.11-3); if the slot ends while the driver is still planning, the Slide disables and the Estimate reads "Your slot has ended." with "Book a slot".
23. **Dynamic Type (AX sizes)** → the hero number caps at Display/XL ×1.2; the panel content scrolls above the pinned action; chips wrap to two rows.
24. **Small phones (375×667)** → hero shrinks to Display/L and the panel starts at y 248; same order.
25. **Status-bar clock** → frames override it to the scenario time where the component exposes the text (listed per frame); otherwise 9:41.
26. **Rounding** → kWh 1 decimal, Rs whole rupees, % whole with "≈". 6.05 kWh shows "6.1".
27. **Haptics off / Low Power Mode** → no pulses or breathing loops; all state changes still visible.
28. **Bookings unavailable on the Charge tab** (offline, request failed) → Next booking card hidden, logic State 1 shown, scanning still allowed (the booking check runs after the scan and gives its own errors) [CH25].
29. **Start request slow** → Starting state for up to 15 s, then the failure path (4.12-5/6); a late success is picked up by the shell's session hydration (edge 21).
30. **Tab bar while the planner or scanner is open** → hidden (full-screen modal / pushed scanner); the accessory never shows over the planner or Connecting.
